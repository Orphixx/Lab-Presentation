The Ten Habits

Click any habit to learn more

1. Drink Water – Start your morning with a glass of water. 2. Exercise – Move your body for 30 minutes a day. 3. Eat Healthy – Choose fresh food over junk food. 4. Sleep Well – Get 7 to 8 hours of sleep every night. 5. Meditate – Take 5 minutes to breathe and relax. 6. No Phone Before Bed – Avoid screens one hour before sleeping. 7. Be Grateful – Write 3 things you are thankful for. 8. Get Sunlight – Spend 15 minutes outside every day. 9. Talk to Friends – Spend time with people you love. 10. Learn New Things – Read or try a new skill each day.
