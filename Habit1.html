Habit 1: Drink Water 💧

💧

Our team practicing this habit 💚

Why It Matters

Drinking water first thing in the morning rehydrates your body after sleep, boosts your metabolism, and helps your brain wake up.

How To Do It

Keep a glass or bottle of water next to your bed. As soon as you wake up, drink one full glass before touching your phone.

Quick Tip

Add a slice of lemon or cucumber for flavor and extra vitamins.
