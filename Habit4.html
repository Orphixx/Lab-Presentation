Habit 4: Sleep Well 😴

😴

Our team practicing this habit 💚

Why It Matters

Sleep repairs your body and clears your mind. Without it, you feel tired, cranky, and can't focus.

How To Do It

Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day — even on weekends. Aim for 7 to 8 hours.

Quick Tip

Keep your room cool, dark, and quiet. Avoid caffeine after 2 PM.
