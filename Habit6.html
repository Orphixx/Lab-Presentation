Habit 6: No Phone Before Bed 📵

📵

Our team practicing this habit 💚

Why It Matters

Screen light tricks your brain into thinking it's still daytime. This makes it harder to fall asleep and lowers sleep quality.

How To Do It

Put your phone in another room (or across the room) one hour before bed. Read a book, stretch, or journal instead.

Quick Tip

If you use your phone as an alarm, buy a cheap alarm clock so you don't need your phone at night.
