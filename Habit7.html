Habit 7: Be Grateful 🙏

🙏

Our team practicing this habit 💚

Why It Matters

Gratitude rewires your brain to notice the good things. It boosts happiness and reduces stress.

How To Do It

Write down 3 things you're thankful for every night before bed. Be specific.

Quick Tip

"My friend's laugh" beats "my friends." Small details make it more powerful.
