Habit 9: Talk to Friends 👥

👥

Our team practicing this habit 💚

Why It Matters

Strong relationships are one of the biggest predictors of happiness and long life. Connection matters.

How To Do It

Call or meet someone you care about at least once a week. Send a quick text to check in.

Quick Tip

A short message counts. "Thinking of you" can make someone's day.
